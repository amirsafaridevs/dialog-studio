## E-commerce / Product-focused design style

**Feel it should produce:** frictionless browsing and confident buying — every screen should reduce hesitation to purchase.

**Core rules:**
- Product grid: consistent card aspect ratio, product image fills most of the card, price + name always visible without hover-reveal on mobile, hover on desktop can reveal a quick "Add to cart" affordance.
- Product image is the hero of every card and the PDP — invest layout space there; secondary info (rating, price, badges) stays compact and consistent position across all cards so the eye scans predictably.
- Price hierarchy: sale price larger/bolder than the struck-through original; currency consistent; avoid ambiguous discount math — show the real savings.
- PDP (product detail page) structure, top to bottom: gallery (left/large) + title, price, rating, short value bullets, variant selectors (size/color as visible swatches not just a dropdown), quantity, prominent "Add to cart" button (highest contrast on the page), trust row (shipping/returns/secure payment icons), then description/reviews below the fold.
- Cart/checkout: minimize steps, show order summary persistently, never hide shipping cost until the last step, guest checkout allowed, progress indicator if multi-step.
- Trust signals throughout: real review counts/stars, secure-payment badges, clear return policy link, stock/urgency indicators used honestly (never fake countdown timers).
- Mobile: sticky "Add to cart" bar on PDP scroll, filter/sort as a bottom sheet not a sidebar.

**Anti-patterns to avoid:** hiding price until a click, tiny/ambiguous "Add to cart" buttons, product grids with inconsistent card heights (misaligned masonry), surprise costs at final checkout step, fake urgency/scarcity.
