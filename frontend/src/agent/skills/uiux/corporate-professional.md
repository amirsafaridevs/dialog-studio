## Corporate / Professional design style

**Feel it should produce:** competence and trust. The visitor should believe this company is stable and capable before reading a word of copy.

**Core rules:**
- Palette: one deep "brand" color (navy, deep blue, deep green, or charcoal) as primary, a light neutral background (white/off-white), a single accent (often warm — orange/gold) reserved for CTAs only. Avoid more than 3 colors total.
- Typography: a sturdy sans-serif (system stack: -apple-system, Segoe UI, Roboto, Helvetica) for both headings and body; do NOT use playful/rounded/display fonts. Headings bold and decisive, body legible at 16–18px.
- Structure over cleverness: hero with clear value proposition + one primary CTA, logo bar (client/partner trust signals), services/features in a clean 3-column grid, testimonials with real names/titles/photos, clear footer with contact + legal links.
- Cards with subtle borders or very light shadows (not heavy neumorphic shadows) to separate sections — order and hierarchy over decoration.
- Icons: simple line icons, consistent stroke width, never mismatched icon sets.
- CTA buttons: solid fill, high contrast, unambiguous label ("Request a Quote", "Book a Consultation" — never vague "Click here").
- Forms: clearly labeled, inline validation, visible required-field markers.

**Anti-patterns to avoid:** neon/bright playful colors, hand-drawn illustrations, casual slang copy, excessive animation, meme-like imagery, cluttered hero with 3+ competing CTAs.
