## SaaS / Startup landing page design style

**Feel it should produce:** clarity and momentum — a visitor should understand what the product does and why it matters within 5 seconds, then be guided smoothly to sign up.

**Core rules:**
- Hero formula: one-sentence value proposition (outcome, not feature list) as the H1, one supporting sentence, ONE primary CTA button + optional secondary ghost-button ("See how it works"), product screenshot/mockup or short demo visual to the side or below.
- Palette: one brand primary color for CTAs/links, a light neutral background, optional gradient accent in the hero background only — keep the rest of the page calm so the CTA color stands out every time it appears.
- Social proof directly under the hero: logo bar of customers/press, or a single strong stat ("10,000+ teams", "4.9/5 rating") — build trust before explaining features.
- Feature sections alternate layout (image-left/text-right, then flipped) to keep scroll rhythm; each section makes ONE claim with a short supporting screenshot, not a wall of bullet points.
- Pricing section: 3 tiers max, the recommended tier visually emphasized (border/scale/badge), feature comparison legible at a glance, monthly/annual toggle if relevant.
- Final CTA section near the footer: repeat the primary action once more with minimal friction ("Start free trial — no credit card required").
- Sticky nav with the primary CTA always visible/reachable.

**Anti-patterns to avoid:** vague hero copy ("The best platform for everything"), more than one primary-styled CTA competing for attention, feature lists with no visual support, pricing tables with all tiers looking equally weighted, burying the CTA below multiple scrolls.
