## Playful / Friendly design style

**Feel it should produce:** approachable, warm, non-intimidating — the opposite of corporate formality.

**Core rules:**
- Palette: bright, saturated but well-balanced colors (2–3 primary brand colors + a light neutral background) — avoid muddy/desaturated tones, this style wants to feel alive.
- Shapes: rounded corners everywhere (12px+), soft blob shapes as background decoration, circular avatars/icons, no sharp edges.
- Typography: a friendly rounded or slightly quirky sans-serif for headings (still legible, not a novelty script font), comfortable sans for body.
- Illustration over photography where possible: custom or consistent-style illustrations, simple character/mascot elements, hand-drawn accents (squiggly underlines, doodle arrows) used purposefully to guide attention.
- Buttons: chunky, rounded, often with a playful hover (slight bounce/scale — respecting prefers-reduced-motion), bright fill colors.
- Microcopy tone matches visuals: friendly, encouraging, conversational — this is a content cue for the copy the agent writes into templates, not just CSS.
- Generous rounded cards with soft colored shadows (colored, not gray/black) matching the brand palette.

**Anti-patterns to avoid:** applying to trust-critical contexts (banking, legal, healthcare data) where playful undermines credibility, sharp corners mixed in inconsistently, stock corporate photography clashing with illustration, over-animating so it feels chaotic rather than delightful.
