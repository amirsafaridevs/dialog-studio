## Dark / Neon-accent modern design style

**Feel it should produce:** cutting-edge, technical, energetic. Common for developer tools, AI products, gaming, Web3.

**Core rules:**
- Background: near-black or deep charcoal/navy (#0a0a0f–#111827 range), never pure #000 (crushes contrast) — layer 2–3 shades of dark for depth (page bg < card bg < raised element).
- ONE neon/vivid accent (electric blue, purple/violet, or green) used for links, primary buttons, glows, and chart highlights — never more than one hero accent, a second color only as a rare complementary highlight.
- Text: off-white (#e5e7eb-ish), never pure white on pure black; secondary text muted gray (#9ca3af-ish). Contrast ≥ 4.5:1 still applies.
- Glow effects used sparingly and purposefully: subtle box-shadow with the accent color at low opacity around primary CTAs or active states — not on every element.
- Gradients: subtle diagonal or radial gradients in hero backgrounds/cards (dark-to-darker, or accent-to-transparent), never loud rainbow gradients.
- Cards: slightly lighter than page background, thin 1px border in a low-opacity light color (rgba(255,255,255,0.08)) instead of heavy shadows.
- Typography: modern geometric sans (Inter, Space Grotesk-like stacks), tight tracking on large display headings.
- Code/mono accents (for dev tools): monospace snippets in a slightly different dark shade with syntax-highlight-like accent coloring.

**Anti-patterns to avoid:** pure black + pure white together (harsh), multiple neon colors competing, glow on every button (loses hierarchy), low-contrast gray-on-gray text, light-mode assumptions (dark shadows, black text) leaking in.
