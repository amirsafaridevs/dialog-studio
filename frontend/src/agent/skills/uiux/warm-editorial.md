## Warm / Editorial (magazine) design style

**Feel it should produce:** inviting, human, story-driven — like reading a well-designed print magazine.

**Core rules:**
- Background: warm off-white/cream (#faf7f2-ish), never stark white or gray. Text: warm near-black (#2a2420-ish), not pure black.
- Typography pairing: a serif for headlines (editorial, high-contrast strokes) + a clean sans for body/UI — exactly this pairing, don't use serif for both. Pull quotes in larger italic serif.
- Accent color drawn from the subject matter (terracotta/rust for food, sage for wellness, deep burgundy for culture) — one accent, used on links, category tags, and small details.
- Layout: asymmetric editorial grids — large hero image bleeding to one edge, text column offset rather than dead-centered, pull quotes breaking the grid intentionally.
- Imagery-first: large, high-quality photography does the emotional work; captions in small italic serif under images.
- Article cards: image + category tag (small pill) + serif headline + one-line excerpt + author/date in muted small text — no heavy borders, separation via spacing and a thin hairline rule.
- Buttons/links: understated — often just underlined text links in the accent color rather than boxed buttons, for a literary feel.

**Anti-patterns to avoid:** stark white backgrounds, sans-serif for headlines, centered-everything layouts, stock-photo grids with no hierarchy, corporate blue accents.
