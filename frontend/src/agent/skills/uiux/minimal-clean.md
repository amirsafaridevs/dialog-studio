## Minimal / Clean design style

**Feel it should produce:** calm, confident, premium. The content is the interface — chrome gets out of the way.

**Core rules:**
- Negative space is a design element, not leftover space. When in doubt, remove — don't add.
- One typeface family, two weights max (regular + bold/medium). Generous line-height (1.5–1.7 body).
- Near-monochrome palette: one neutral background, one near-black text color, ONE accent used sparingly (links, primary CTA, focus states only).
- No shadows, no gradients, no decorative borders. Separation comes from spacing, not lines/boxes.
- Grid: generous margins (min 5–8% of viewport on desktop), consistent column gaps, content rarely exceeds ~1100–1200px reading width.
- Buttons: flat or outline, sharp or barely-rounded corners (0–6px), no heavy drop shadows.
- Imagery: large, full-bleed or precisely cropped, never cluttered with overlays/badges.
- Motion: subtle fades/opacity only, 150–250ms, nothing bouncy.

**Anti-patterns to avoid:** busy backgrounds, multiple accent colors, boxed "cards" for everything, icon soup, gradient buttons, drop shadows on every element.
