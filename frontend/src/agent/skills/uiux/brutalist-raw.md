## Brutalist / Raw design style

**Feel it should produce:** confident, unpolished-on-purpose, memorable. Signals "we don't need to look like everyone else."

**Core rules:**
- High-contrast palette: pure black + pure white as the base, ONE loud accent (red, acid yellow/green, or hot pink) used boldly and unapologetically — not subtly.
- Typography as a structural element: very large, often default/system or monospace fonts, tight or intentionally awkward spacing, mixed weights for emphasis, sometimes text overlapping images on purpose.
- Visible structure: exposed grid lines, thick borders (2–4px solid black), no rounded corners, no soft shadows.
- Layout breaks convention deliberately: asymmetric, elements touching edges, overlapping blocks — but hierarchy still works (this is controlled chaos, not random).
- Buttons: rectangular, thick border, hard hover state (invert colors on hover, no smooth easing needed — instant or near-instant transitions fit the aesthetic).
- Imagery: often high-contrast, duotone, or grainy treatment rather than polished stock photography.
- Motion: abrupt/snappy rather than smooth eased transitions — this is intentional, matches the raw aesthetic.

**Anti-patterns to avoid:** applying this to corporate/trust-driven sites (banks, healthcare, B2B SaaS) where it undermines credibility, using it as an excuse for poor hierarchy or unreadable contrast, softening it with rounded corners/shadows (defeats the point).
